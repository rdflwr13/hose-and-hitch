title=Hose & Hitch [B42]
description=Hose & Hitch adds what I was missing in vanilla Build 42: a generator fed from a barrel of gas, running water in the house after the shutoff, and a second trailer in tow. Everything goes through the game's own menus, no new windows. Works in singleplayer and on servers.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/banner_fuel_line_en.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_generator_barrel_r.gif[/img]
description=A barrel of gas can be connected to a generator with a rubber hose: right-click the generator or the barrel, [b]Connect Fuel Barrel[/b]. If the barrel stands higher than the generator, on a crate, a table or the floor above, gas flows in by itself. On the ground, pour it by hand with the generator off: [b]Pour Fuel from the Barrel[/b].
description=Keep the barrel closed: in an open one gas evaporates, and rain spoils it.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/banner_water_supply_en.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_hoist_barrel_r.gif[/img]
description=A barrel can be hoisted onto the roof: stand by the house wall with a barrel, a rope and a pipe wrench and pick [b]Hoist Barrel onto the Roof[/b]. Rain fills the barrel, and after the water shutoff it feeds every tap in the house below: sink, bath, shower and toilet. Right-click a tap to see how much is left. A barrel on the top floor of the house can be connected with the wrench too.
description=[h3]Roofs[/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*]You can't climb a sloped roof, so a barrel only goes up there from the ground.
description=[*]On a flat roof you can carry a barrel up or hoist it from the ground, but a hoisted barrel isn't visible there.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/banner_double_tow_en.png[/img]

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_hitch_vans_r.gif[/img]
description=A second trailer can be hitched behind the first, and behind a towed car, another trailer or car. Park it right behind, stand at the back and pick [b]Hitch on the drawbar[/b] in the vehicle menu ([b]V[/b]). Hitch in any order: the mod gives the front pair the game's own hitch. A heavy single-axle trailer keeps its nose at hitch height instead of dragging on the ground.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/banner_barrels_trunks_en.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Any barrel, not just a rain collector, holds 400 L, and it can stand on a crate, a table or an orange barrel.
description=[*]Trunks, truck beds and trailers hold twice as much: a real trailer carries hundreds of kilos, not a hundred.
description=[*]Roof racks, rear racks and bags from Standardized Vehicle Upgrades 3 and PZK tuning take a quarter more: a roof can't hold much.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3217685049]PZK Vanilla Plus Car Pack[/url]: made and tested with it. Its trailers, tankers and campers hitch on the drawbar, and its beds and trunks hold more.
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3304582091]Standardized Vehicle Upgrades 3[/url]: roof racks, rear racks and bags take a quarter more.
description=[*]Cars and trailers from other mods work if they use the game's usual hitch and trunk. Not all of them are tested.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]FAQ[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Can I add it to an existing save?[/b] Yes, no new game needed.
description=[*][b]Can I remove it?[/b] Yes. Generators and taps work like vanilla again, and whatever is in the barrels stays.
description=[*][b]Does the generator refuel while I'm away?[/b] While the area is loaded, yes, like everything else in the game.
description=[*][b]No water at the tap.[/b] Water only flows down, so the tap has to be lower than the barrel. Only the taps the house was built with work; a sink you moved isn't fed.
description=[*][b]Is the tap water drinkable?[/b] Yes, same as vanilla plumbing.
description=[*][b]Why is a menu option red?[/b] Something's missing, and the tooltip says what.
description=[/list]
