version=1
title=Hose & Hitch [B42]
description=Things I miss in vanilla Build 42, in one mod. Everything goes through the game's own menus.
description=I didn't want a separate mod for every little thing, so I'm putting what I miss in vanilla into one mod and will keep adding to it. I keep it close to the base game: realistic, without extra hassle.
description=Ideas are welcome.
description=Tested on a dedicated server and in singleplayer.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/banner_fuel_line_en.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_generator_barrel.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]A barrel of gas within 2 tiles of the generator and a rubber hose. Right-click the generator or the barrel: [b]Connect Fuel Barrel[/b].
description=[*]Barrel higher than the generator (crate, table, floor above): gas flows by itself. Barrel on the ground: [b]Pour Fuel from the Barrel[/b] with the generator off.
description=[*]In an open barrel gas evaporates, and rain spoils it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/banner_water_supply_en.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_hoist_barrel.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Stand outside, up to 2 tiles from the wall, with a barrel, a rope and a pipe wrench: [b]Hoist Barrel onto the Roof[/b]. Rain fills it.
description=[*]Once the water is shut off, sinks, baths, showers and toilets below get water from the roof barrels.
description=[*]A barrel on an upper floor indoors can be connected with a pipe wrench. Right-click a tap to see how much water is left.
description=[*]To take a barrel back down: [b]Lower Barrel from the Roof[/b], outside by the wall.
description=[/list]
description=[h3]Limitations[/h3]
description=[list]
description=[*]Sloped roof: you can't climb onto it, so the barrel can only be hoisted from the ground.
description=[*]Flat roof: you can carry a barrel up and place it by hand, or hoist it from the ground. But a barrel hoisted from the ground isn't visible on a flat roof.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/banner_double_tow_en.png[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_hitch_pickups.gif[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_hitch_camper_tank.gif[/img]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rdflwr13/hose-and-hitch/main/images/gif_hitch_vans.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Behind a towed car you can hitch a trailer or another car. Behind a trailer, another trailer.
description=[*]Park it right behind, stand at the back of the towed one and open the vehicle menu ([b]V[/b]): [b]Hitch on the drawbar[/b].
description=[*]Hitch in any order: if you hitch a car to a pair that's already towing, the mod gives the front pair the game's own hitch and puts the drawbar at the back.
description=[*]A heavy single-axle trailer keeps its nose up at hitch height instead of dragging on the ground.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Also[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]A barrel goes on a crate, a table or an orange barrel.
description=[*]Any barrel holds 400 L.
description=[*]Car trunks, truck beds and trailer beds hold twice as much.
description=[*]Roof racks and rear racks from the tuning mod hold a quarter more: a roof can't take much.
description=[*]Bigger trunks work on modded vehicles with a regular trunk too.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How to use[/h2]
description=Everything is in the right-click menu. If you're missing something, the option is red and the tooltip says what you need.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Don't use it together with [b]Knox Water[/b]: both mods feed house taps from roof barrels and will get in each other's way.
description=[*]Works with modded cars and trailers that use the game's usual hitch.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Languages[/h2]
description=Every language in the game. Not translated by native speakers, so if something reads wrong, let me know.
description=
description=[h2]FAQ[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Can I add it to an existing save?[/b] Yes, no new game needed.
description=[*][b]Can I remove it?[/b] Yes. Generators and taps work like vanilla again, and whatever is in the barrels stays.
description=[*][b]Does the generator refuel while I'm away?[/b] While the area is loaded, yes, same as vanilla.
description=[*][b]No water at the tap.[/b] Water only flows down: the tap has to be lower than the barrel. Only the taps the house was built with work. A sink you moved isn't fed by the barrels.
description=[*][b]Is the tap water drinkable?[/b] Yes, same as vanilla plumbing.
description=[/list]
